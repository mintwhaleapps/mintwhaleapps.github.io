Passnest

Your family's passports, visas and IDs. Encrypted on your iPhone, ready at the border, even with no signal. No account and no cloud.

What it does

  • Scans passports and IDs and fills in the details for you
  • Reminds you before anything expires, for the whole family
  • Checks everyone's passport and visas against your trip dates
  • Shows a document full screen at a counter, and helps if a passport is lost
  • Safe Share: watermark copies with who they're for and hide numbers
  • Finds ID photos sitting in your camera roll and moves them into the vault

Pricing

Free for one person and up to 5 documents. Passnest Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited documents, family profiles and Safe Share. No subscription.
