Gigabye

Say bye to wasted gigabytes. Find and clear the clutter filling your iPhone: chat forwards, old screenshots, similar shots, repeated images and large videos. Everything happens on your iPhone.

What it does

  • Groups saved & received images by source (WhatsApp, Telegram and more)
  • Finds similar shots and keeps the best one
  • Sorts screenshots and spots expired tickets and old OTPs
  • Finds large videos and compresses them
  • Smart tools: Privacy sweep, Live Photos to stills, Documents to PDF

Pricing

Free: 15 deletions a month. Unlimited and Complete are one-time purchases. No subscription.
