ProofPin

GPS timestamp camera for work. Date, time, full address and GPS on every photo, then a PDF report in one tap. No ads, no account, and your photos stay on your iPhone.

What it does

  • Stamps every photo with the date, time, street address, GPS coordinates and their accuracy
  • Four stamp styles, including a mini map with a pin
  • Wrong address? Pick a nearby place or type it, free. Coordinates never change and the stamp shows the edit
  • Works with no signal: the address is added when you're back online
  • Organises photos by job and sends a PDF report with a map and a signature line
  • Keeps the untouched original of every photo

Pricing

Stamping is free and unlimited, with no ads. ProofPin Pro adds your logo, unlimited projects and reports of any length: monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase. No weekly plans.

Also in German, French and Spanish.
