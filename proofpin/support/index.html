Common questions

The address on a photo is wrong.

After taking a photo, tap Wrong? Fix it, or open the photo and tap Fix address. Pick a nearby place, search, or type the address. Fixing an address is always free. The GPS coordinates stay exactly as measured, and the stamp shows that the address was edited. To set the address for the photos you're about to take, tap the stamp on the camera screen.

I have no signal.

ProofPin still stamps the date, time and GPS coordinates. The address (and the map, on a Map stamp) is added automatically when your iPhone is back online.

Where are my photos?

In ProofPin, under the project you took them in: tap the square button next to the shutter. To also keep a copy in Photos, open Settings in ProofPin and turn on Also save to Photos. ProofPin always keeps the untouched original as well as the stamped copy.

How do I send a report?

Open a project and tap Create PDF report, then Send report. Free reports hold 6 photos and carry a small ProofPin footer. With ProofPin Pro, reports hold any number of photos and carry your logo instead. To send the photos themselves, use Share photos on the project screen.

Can I take photos with the volume buttons?

Yes. On the camera screen, press either volume button to take the photo.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions › ProofPin. Stamping stays free after you cancel.

I got a new iPhone. How do I get Pro back?

Install ProofPin, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I change the language?

ProofPin follows your iPhone's language (English, German, French or Spanish). To use a different one for ProofPin only, open iPhone Settings › Apps › ProofPin › Language.
