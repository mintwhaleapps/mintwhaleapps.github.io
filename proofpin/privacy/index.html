Privacy Policy

ProofPin by Mintwhale · Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version

Your photos stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising, and we collect nothing.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photos, the stamped copies, your projects, notes, stamp text, settings and logo are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are not uploaded to us or anyone else. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it, unless you saved copies to Photos or sent them somewhere yourself.

Camera, location and Photos

Camera is used to take the photos you stamp.

Location is used only while the app is open, to print the address and GPS coordinates on your photos.

Photos: if you turn on "Also save to Photos", ProofPin adds stamped photos to your library; it cannot read your library. When you add photos from your library, you choose them in Apple's photo picker and only those photos are shared with the app.

Addresses and maps

To turn GPS coordinates into a street address, to suggest nearby places, and to draw the map on a Map stamp or in a report, ProofPin sends the coordinates to Apple's Maps service. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy. No photo, name or account is sent with them.

What we collect

Nothing. ProofPin has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not receive your photos, your location or anything about how you use the app.

Sharing

Photos and reports leave your iPhone only when you share them yourself, for example by email or to Files. Once shared, they are handled by the app or person you sent them to.

Purchases

ProofPin Pro is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Feedback you send us

If you email us or send a suggestion, we receive what you choose to write and your email address. We use it only to reply and to improve our apps, and we never sell or share it.

Children

ProofPin is a work tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email mintwhale.apps@gmail.com and mention ProofPin in your message.
