Common questions

Why don't I see a health score yet?

The health score compares your readings with what the weather allowed at your home. It needs your town (Protect › Expected vs actual › Your roof) and at least 3 days of daily readings. After about 10 readings, the app learns your system's own efficiency.

Which number should I log?

Log the energy your system made in one whole day, in kWh. Many inverters and solar apps call it "Today", "E-Day" or "Daily yield". Don't use the lifetime total. Before 6 pm the app suggests logging yesterday's total, because today isn't finished yet. You can also log a monthly total.

Can I log a reading from a photo or with Siri?

Yes. Tap the + button, then photograph your inverter or meter, or choose a screenshot from your solar app. The app reads the numbers on your iPhone and you tap the right one. With Siri, say "Log solar in SunPanel Log".

I logged the wrong number.

Log the same day again with the right number. The app asks before it replaces the earlier reading. You can also delete a reading in the Energy tab by pressing and holding it.

The app says my system is below expected.

The score is an estimate, not a measurement. First check the reading is a full day's total and that your system size (kW), town and roof direction are right. If it stays low, check the inverter for an error, look for new shade or dirt, and ask a qualified installer to inspect the system. Never climb on the roof yourself.

How do I move my records to a new iPhone?

In SunPanel Log, open Settings › Export full backup and save the file somewhere private, such as Files or iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, install the app and tap I have a SunPanel Log backup, or use Settings › Restore from backup.

How does the free trial work, and how do I cancel?

The yearly plan can start with a 7-day free trial. If you allow notifications, the app reminds you 2 days before the trial ends. To cancel a trial or subscription, open your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions › SunPanel Log. Your records stay in the app after you cancel.

I got a new iPhone. How do I get Plus back?

Install SunPanel Log, open the Plus screen and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account. This works for subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase.

Does the app connect to my inverter?

No. SunPanel Log works without an account or an inverter connection, so it works with any brand. You enter readings yourself, from a photo, from a CSV file or with Siri.

SunPanel Log helps you keep records and spot problems early. Estimates and claim packs are based on what you enter and on weather data, and are not professional advice or an inspection. Always use a qualified installer for repairs, and check your warranty and insurance terms.
