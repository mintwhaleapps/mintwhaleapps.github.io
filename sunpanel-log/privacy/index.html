Privacy Policy

SunPanel Log by Mintwhale · Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

Your solar records stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising, and we collect nothing.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter or add in SunPanel Log is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. That includes your system details, readings, service history, reminders, warranties, guarantee terms, bills, outages, check-ups, damage reports, photos and the documents you import. None of it is uploaded to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, or use Settings › Delete all local data in the app, it is deleted from your iPhone.

Location

SunPanel Log uses your location only while the app is open, and only when you ask it to:

  • Finding your town. When you type a town or postcode, or tap Use my location, the app looks up the place with Apple's location service so it can work out local weather for your panels. The app keeps the town name and its approximate coordinates on your iPhone.
  • Damage photos. In Storm & damage, photos you take in the app can be stamped with the date, time and GPS position, so an insurer can see where and when they were taken.

Weather

To estimate how much your panels should produce, and to suggest the sunniest hours, SunPanel Log asks Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the weather at your system's approximate location. Only the coordinates are sent, with no name, photo or reading. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather (data sources).

Camera, photos and text recognition

Camera is used to photograph your inverter or meter display, and to take damage, check-up and service photos.

Reading numbers from a photo (Snap to log) happens entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo is not uploaded.

Photos: when you add a picture from your library, you choose it in Apple's photo picker and only that picture is shared with the app.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you say "Log solar in SunPanel Log", Siri passes the number you speak to the app, which saves it on your iPhone. Your voice request is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders, warranty alerts, sunny-hours tips and the free-trial reminder are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through a server.

What we collect

Nothing. SunPanel Log has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not receive your readings, photos, documents, location or anything about how you use the app.

Sharing and backups

Your records leave your iPhone only when you share them yourself, for example a Solar Passport or claim pack PDF, a CSV export or a full backup file sent by email or saved to Files. Once shared, they are handled by the app or person you sent them to. A backup file contains all your records and attachments, so keep it somewhere private.

Purchases

SunPanel Log Plus subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Feedback you send us

If you email us or send a suggestion, we receive what you choose to write and your email address. We use it only to reply and to improve our apps, and we never sell or share it.

Children

SunPanel Log is made for home owners and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
