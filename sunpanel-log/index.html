SunPanel Log

Know your solar is working, and have the proof when it isn't. No account, no inverter connection, and your records stay on your iPhone.

What it does

  • A health score that compares your readings with what the weather allowed at your home
  • Log a reading in seconds: type it, photograph the inverter, or ask Siri
  • Warranties, service history, documents and a shareable Solar Passport in one place
  • Guarantee tracking and storm, outage and damage claim packs
  • Tomorrow's sunniest hours, so you can use your own power
  • Works with any brand of panels and inverter

Pricing

Logging, the health score, check-ups and reminders are free. SunPanel Log Plus adds claim packs, full history, PDF reports and unlimited documents and systems: monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase. No weekly plans.
